## Evaluation Metrics

Common evaluation measures identified from the reviewed studies include:

- Accuracy
- Precision
- Recall
- F1-score
- False Positive Rate (FPR)
- Sensitivity
- Specificity
- AUC
- Mean Squared Error (MSE)
- Global model accuracy
- Processing latency

## Relevance to Proposed Research

These datasets and evaluation metrics provide possible references for designing the evaluation of the proposed machine-learning-based detection module.

The final dataset and evaluation setup should be selected based on the proposed methodology and research objectives.
