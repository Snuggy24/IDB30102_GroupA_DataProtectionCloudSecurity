# Pacheco (2024)

## Paper Title

A Comprehensive Survey on Federated Learning and Its Applications in Health Care

## Author

Pacheco

## Year

2024

## Research Problem

The study provides an overview of Federated Learning and its applications in healthcare, including privacy and security considerations.

## Method / Technique

The study is a comprehensive survey of Federated Learning concepts, applications, privacy issues and security challenges.

## Dataset / Tools

- Federated Learning literature
- Healthcare applications
- Privacy and security mechanisms

## Main Findings

The survey highlights the importance of privacy and security when Federated Learning is applied to sensitive healthcare data.

## Limitation

As a survey study, it does not provide a dedicated experimental machine-learning-based poisoning detection system.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

This study provides background support for the importance of security in Federated Learning. It helps establish why protecting client contributions and the global model is important in sensitive cloud-based environments.
