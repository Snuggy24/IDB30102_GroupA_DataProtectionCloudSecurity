# Zhu, Huang & Zhang (2026)

## Paper Title

Statistical test-based adversarial client detection in federated learning under poisoning attacks

## Authors

Zhu, Huang & Zhang

## Year

2026

## Research Problem

The study investigates the detection of adversarial clients in Federated Learning under poisoning attacks.

## Method / Technique

The proposed FLGMM approach combines Gaussian Mixture Models (GMM) with statistical process control techniques to identify suspicious client behaviour.

## Dataset / Tools

- MNIST
- FashionMNIST
- CIFAR-10
- Shakespeare
- Gaussian Mixture Model
- Statistical Process Control

## Evaluation Metrics

- Global model accuracy
- False Positive Rate (FPR)

## Main Findings

The study reported improvements in global model accuracy ranging from approximately 0.02% to 46.82%, with a reported false positive rate of around 0.135%.

## Limitation

The approach depends on statistical modelling and monitoring assumptions. Further evaluation is needed to determine its suitability for a practical cloud-based detection module.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

This study demonstrates the value of monitoring client behaviour statistically across Federated Learning rounds. It provides a comparison point for the proposed machine-learning-based detection approach.
