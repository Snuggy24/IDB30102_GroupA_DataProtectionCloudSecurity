# Ma, Zhao & Tian (2025)

## Paper Title

A Defense Method Against Multi-Label Poisoning Attacks in Federated Learning

## Authors

Ma, Zhao & Tian

## Year

2025

## Research Problem

The study focuses on multi-label poisoning attacks in Federated Learning and investigates methods for identifying malicious clients.

## Method / Technique

The method analyses gradients from the output layer and applies clustering to identify suspicious client updates.

## Dataset / Tools

- MNIST
- FashionMNIST
- NSL-KDD
- CICIDS-2017
- Output-layer gradients
- Clustering

## Main Findings

The study demonstrates that output-layer gradient information can be used to distinguish clients affected by multi-label poisoning attacks.

## Limitation

The method is designed specifically for multi-label poisoning attacks and may require further adaptation for other poisoning scenarios.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

The study provides evidence that gradient-based features can support malicious-client detection. It also highlights the importance of selecting suitable features for a machine-learning-based detection module.
