# Lai et al. (2023)

## Paper Title

Two-phase Defense Against Poisoning Attacks on Federated Learning-based Intrusion Detection

## Authors

Lai et al.

## Year

2023

## Research Problem

The study addresses poisoning attacks that can reduce the performance of Federated Learning-based intrusion detection systems.

## Method / Technique

The study proposes a two-phase defense mechanism called DPA-FL. It uses relative weight differences to identify suspicious client updates and then applies accuracy testing to further evaluate the updates.

## Dataset / Tools

- Federated Learning-based intrusion detection
- Label-flipping attacks
- Backdoor attacks
- Client model updates
- Relative weight differences
- Accuracy testing

## Main Findings

The proposed defense achieved approximately 96.5% defense accuracy against the evaluated poisoning attacks.

## Limitation

The evaluation mainly focused on label-flipping and backdoor attacks. The approach may require additional testing against other poisoning behaviours and more diverse Federated Learning environments.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

This study shows that client model updates can be examined before aggregation to identify suspicious behaviour. It supports the idea of analysing client updates as input to a detection module.
