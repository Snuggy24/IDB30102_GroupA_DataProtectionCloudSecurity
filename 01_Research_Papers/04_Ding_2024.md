# Ding et al. (2024)

## Paper Title

Identifying alternately poisoning attacks in federated learning online using trajectory anomaly detection method

## Authors

Ding et al.

## Year

2024

## Research Problem

The study investigates poisoning behaviour that changes client model parameters over multiple Federated Learning rounds.

## Method / Technique

The method extracts singular-value-based features from client updates and applies an improved Isolation Forest to detect anomalous client behaviour.

## Dataset / Tools

- MNIST
- FashionMNIST
- CIFAR-10
- Client model updates
- Singular-value features
- Improved Isolation Forest

## Main Findings

The method achieved approximately 94.3% detection accuracy with a false positive rate below 1.2% in the reported experiments.

## Limitation

The approach focuses on a particular poisoning behaviour and relies on trajectory-based anomaly features. Further evaluation may be required for different attack types and cloud-based Federated Learning environments.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

This study is highly relevant because it demonstrates the use of anomaly detection on client updates. It provides a basis for investigating machine-learning-based detection of abnormal client behaviour.
