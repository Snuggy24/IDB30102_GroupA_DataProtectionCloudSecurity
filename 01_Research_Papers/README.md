# Research Papers

This folder contains the research papers and research-paper analysis supporting Chapter 2: Literature Review.

The selected studies focus on Federated Learning, data poisoning attacks, malicious client updates, anomaly detection, clustering-based detection, statistical detection and machine-learning-based security mechanisms.

7. Ma, Zhao & Tian (2025)
8. Zhu, Huang & Zhang (2026)
9. Pacheco (2024)
10. Raju et al. (2026)

Each paper is analysed based on:

- Research problem
- Method / technique
- Dataset / tools
- Main findings
- Limitations
- Relevance to the proposed research

The research evidence supports the development of the proposed machine-learning-based detection module for data poisoning attacks in cloud-based Federated Learning.
