# Qayyum, Janjua & Qadir (2022)

## Paper Title

Making Federated Learning Robust to Adversarial Attacks by Learning Data and Model Association

## Authors

Qayyum, Janjua & Qadir

## Year

2022

## Research Problem

The study investigates malicious or poisoned client parameter updates that can negatively affect the global Federated Learning model.

## Method / Technique

The study uses a hybrid learning-based detection approach using data-model association and cosine similarity to identify malicious client updates.

## Dataset / Tools

- MNIST
- CIFAR-10
- APTOS diabetic retinopathy dataset
- Federated Learning
- Machine Learning
- Cosine similarity
- Model-parameter analysis

## Main Findings

The approach was able to detect and discard poisoned parameter updates without significantly reducing the overall Federated Learning performance.

## Limitation

The study was mainly evaluated against label-flipping attacks and specific image-classification tasks.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

This study demonstrates that client model updates can be analysed to identify malicious behaviour without directly accessing private client data. It provides a useful basis for developing the proposed machine-learning-based detection module.
