# Basak & Chatterjee (2025)

## Paper Title

DPAD: Data Poisoning Attack Defense Mechanism for federated learning-based system

## Authors

Basak & Chatterjee

## Year

2025

## Research Problem

The study addresses data poisoning attacks that can affect the performance of Federated Learning-based systems.

## Method / Technique

The proposed DPAD mechanism uses an audit process to verify client updates before they are included in aggregation.

## Dataset / Tools

- Federated Learning
- Client updates
- Data poisoning attacks
- Audit mechanism

## Evaluation Metrics

- Accuracy
- Mean Squared Error (MSE)
- Sensitivity
- Specificity
- Area Under the Curve (AUC)

## Main Findings

The proposed mechanism evaluates client updates before aggregation to reduce the effect of poisoned information on the global model.

## Limitation

The audit process may introduce additional processing requirements, which should be considered when developing a practical detection mechanism.

## Relevance to Proposed Research

This study supports the need to inspect client updates before aggregation. It is relevant to the proposed detection module because malicious updates need to be identified before they can negatively affect the global model.
