## 2. Benchmark Datasets

*   **MNIST Database of Handwritten Digits**
    *   *Reference:* LeCun, Y., Cortes, C., & Burges, C. (2010). The MNIST database of handwritten digits.
    *   *Official Source:* [http://yann.lecun.com/exdb/mnist/](http://yann.lecun.com/exdb/mnist/)
    *   *Usage:* Benchmark image classification dataset used to simulate distributed edge nodes and test label-flipping attacks.

*   **CIFAR-10 Dataset**
    *   *Reference:* Krizhevsky, A., & Hinton, G. (2009). *Learning multiple layers of features from tiny images* (Technical Report). University of Toronto.
    *   *Official Source:* [https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~kriz/cifar.html](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~kriz/cifar.html)
    *   *Usage:* Complex multi-class image benchmark to evaluate weight shift discrepancies under non-IID conditions.

*   **MIMIC-III Clinical Database**
    *   *Reference:* Johnson, A. E., Pollard, T. J., Shen, L., Lehman, L. W., Feng, M., Ghassemi, M., Moody, B., Szolovits, P., Celi, L. A., & Mark, R. G. (2016). MIMIC-III, a freely accessible critical care database. *Scientific Data*, 3, 160035.
    *   *Official Source / DOI:* [https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.35](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.35)
    *   *Usage:* De-identified healthcare time-series records for modeling collaborative cross-silo healthcare consortia.

    *   *Repository:* [https://github.com/CyberData-Security/LFighter](https://github.com/CyberData-Security/LFighter)
